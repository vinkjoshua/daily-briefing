Max 3 items. Papers, engineering write-ups and technical reports from a rolling 7-day window in the research areas in `interests.md`.
Search arXiv and original research or engineering sources; start with the sources in `interests.md` and also discover beyond them. Exclude funding news, generic launch coverage, predictions and tutorials without implementation detail.
Deduplicate by arXiv ID, version, DOI and canonical URL. Screen abstracts first; for shortlisted items read methods, experiments and limitations. If full text is unavailable, say so.
Per item: title, authors or organisation, date, link (plus code link); the problem and how the method works; evidence (baseline, setting, reported improvement); limitations (weak baselines, missing ablations, cost, generalisation); one concrete experiment I could try. Label preprint, production report or independent replication.
