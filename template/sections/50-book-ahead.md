Goal: never miss tickets that sell out early. Track events 1–9 months out (festivals, big concerts, conferences, popular exhibitions) whose tickets or registration open soon or sell out early.
1. Maintain `state/watchlist.md`: verify sale and registration dates, add new events matching `interests.md`, mark past events done, and leave rows I marked `booked` or `skip` alone.
2. In the email, first list every **ACTION** item: sale opens within 14 days, a presale needs registration, or tickets are selling and likely to sell out. Then at most 3 newly added items under "On the radar".
Exception to the dedup rule: ACTION rows repeat every day as a one-line reminder until I mark them `booked` or `skip`, or the deadline passes.
Per item: briefly explain the relevant opportunity or reason to plan ahead; event, event dates, what to do and by when, link.
